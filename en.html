Lei Xiong studies general-purpose multimodal agents for open-ended tasks. The goal is to connect language and visual understanding with planning and tool use, so agents can adapt as new information arrives and deliver results people can verify.

Current work combines system design, evaluation and model post-training to study planning, context management and multi-agent collaboration in long-horizon tasks. Scientific research provides one application, where agents gather evidence across papers, interpret figures and produce reports.

SciResearchBench

Benchmarking AI Agents on Complex Scientific Literature Discovery

Lei Xiong, Kun Luo, Ziyi Xia, Wenbo Zhang, et al.

SciResearchBench tests two demands of scientific search. In one task, an agent follows scattered clues to identify a particular paper. In the other, it must find every study that meets several criteria without knowing how many qualify. A scientific question-answering agent makes it possible to compare search strategies and trace missed leads. The best systems tested reached 9.39% accuracy on paper identification and 9.31% set overlap (IoU) on exhaustive search.

PaperScope

A Multi-Modal Multi-Document Benchmark for Agentic Deep Research Across Massive Scientific Papers

Lei Xiong, Huaying Yuan, Zheng Liu, Zhao Cao, Zhicheng Dou

Interpreting a figure in one paper can require the experimental setup from another. PaperScope links over 2,000 AI papers from three years of research in a knowledge graph and samples related papers to construct over 2,000 questions. Agents must connect text, tables and figures across documents to retrieve information, reason, summarize or solve a problem. The same pipeline can synthesize training data for multimodal research agents.

MR²-Bench

Going Beyond Matching to Reasoning in Multimodal Retrieval

Junjie Zhou, Ze Liu, Lei Xiong, Jin-Ge Yao, et al.

Given a floor plan and an instruction to turn a particular gray object green, which 3D scene shows the result? The object’s position determines the correct image. MR²-Bench brings together 1,309 queries that require spatial, logical or causal reasoning over natural images, diagrams and visual puzzles. Seed1.6-Embedding scored 77.78 Recall@1 on the widely used MMEB benchmark and 9.91 on MR²-Bench.

Research projects

Deep research agent training

AREX-1

Checking a candidate against several requirements is often easier than discovering every qualifying answer from scratch. AREX-1 alternates between those two tasks. It searches for candidates, verifies each requirement and compresses the findings into a record of what is established and what remains open. That record directs the next search toward the missing evidence.

Training starts with verifiable synthetic tasks. Long-horizon reinforcement learning gives extra weight to steps that uncover decisive evidence or correct a false lead. WideSearch training targets exhaustive retrieval and item-by-item verification across sources. AREX-Base reached 82.0 Item-F1 on WideSearch-en.

Decision transfer across agent harnesses

Long-horizon multimodal tasks for agent teams

Agents can lose text and image evidence when they reset context or hand off a long task. ContextScope keeps task progress and source-linked evidence in a shared workspace so agents can resume unfinished work. Training examples come from the model’s attempts to recover from its own mistakes and are verified through execution. The project tests whether decisions learned in a single agent harness transfer to others.

Automated survey writing · Co-first author

SEER

The right structure for a survey often becomes clear only after reading the literature. SEER represents its outline as a tree of research questions. Each agent investigates one branch. When related branches report back, a parent agent can merge overlapping sections, move a misplaced topic or reopen a question that still lacks evidence.

Agents then draft sections in parallel from a shared literature database, keeping claims linked to their sources. With DeepSeek-V4-Flash, SEER scored 9.28 on DeepSurvey Bench, ahead of the strongest external baseline at 8.64. The same configuration earned a RACE score of 57.00 on DeepResearch Bench for open-ended reports.

Literature tools for science agents

DeepXiv-SDK

Verifying an experimental detail may require just one section of a long paper. DeepXiv-SDK lets agents search arXiv, screen abstracts and outlines, then open the experiment or results section that bears on a claim. Its index covers over 2.9 million papers, with more than 2.7 million available for section-by-section reading.

Developers can integrate the same tools through Python, the command line or MCP. On 47 scientific questions that require combining and verifying published evidence, agents using DeepXiv answered more accurately, used fewer tokens and finished sooner than a conventional web search and reading pipeline.
